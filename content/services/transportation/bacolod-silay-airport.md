# Bacolod-Silay International Airport

Find airport information, official airline booking links, and advice for arranging a transfer between Bacolod and Bacolod-Silay Airport.

**Last reviewed:** November 14, 2026

## Airport Information

- **Airport code:** BCD (IATA) / RPVB (ICAO)
- **Location:** Barangay Bagtic, Silay City, Negros Occidental
- **Operator:** Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines (CAAP), Area Center 6

See [CAAP's airport directory](https://www.caap.gov.ph/area-centers/) and its [Area Center VI aerodrome data](https://caap.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/AREA-6.pdf) for published airport information. The aerodrome table is dated February 4, 2022; it is not a current passenger-services or flight schedule.

## Arrange Your Airport Transfer

Confirm your pickup point, destination, fare, luggage allowance, and departure time directly with the provider before travelling. Allow for traffic and waiting time rather than relying on a fixed transfer duration.

- **Shuttle or shared transfer:** Check current availability, pickup locations, and prices with the operator or the airport information desk. This guide does not publish a verified shuttle timetable or fare.
- **Taxi or app booking:** Check availability and the applicable meter information or quoted price before confirming. Do not assume an immediate pickup will be available.
- **Hotel or private transfer:** Agree on the collection time and location with your driver or accommodation, and recheck your flight status before leaving.
- **Public transport via Silay:** Plan the journey and confirm the final connection to the airport before boarding. A route to Silay does not guarantee a direct airport service.

See the [public transport and commute guide](/transportation/check-public-transport-routes-and-schedules) for Lagaw.ph jeepney planning and fare-checking advice.

## Before You Travel

1. Confirm your flight status and the airline's check-in, bag-drop, and boarding deadlines through your booking or the airline's official channels.
2. Check identification, baggage, and other travel requirements with your airline.
3. Arrange your transfer with enough time for traffic, waiting, and airport procedures. Follow your airline's arrival-time advice; check-in opening times are not the same for every flight.
